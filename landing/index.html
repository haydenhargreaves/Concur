What fought back

The hard parts, and how we fixed them.

Vision node

Issue Distinguish whether a person was approaching or moving awaySolution
  • 1.5 seconds of tracking to observe movement
  • 3% per second change in bounding-box size to estimate direction

Movement and light sensors

Issue The sensors initially missed subtle changes in light and motion.Solution
  • Below 50 lux for low-light context
  • 0.05 g for motion
  • 40 mm/s for direction

Dead or just quiet?

Issue A node with no event to report was indistinguishable from a failed node.Solution
  • Heartbeat every 500 milliseconds, even when idle
  • 5 seconds without a heartbeat marks a node offline
